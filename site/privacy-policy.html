Privacy Policy

Module Up: Digital SAT Prep · Android · Last updated 23 October 2026

In short: Module Up keeps your scores and study progress in your account so you can see your progress board and continue on any phone. You can try one practice set as a guest. The app shows ads through Google AdMob. We never sell personal information, and you can delete your account and all study data at any time.

1. Who we are

Module Up is made by HomiLabs ("we", "us"). Contact: homilabs.smc@gmail.com. A summary for all HomiLabs tools is at tools.homilabs.org/privacy.

2. What we collect

WhatWhyWhere it is stored
Your account: email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name and Google account identifierTo sign you in and keep your progress on any phone.Firebase Authentication (Google)
Guest use: an anonymous account identifierSo you can try a practice set before creating an account.Firebase Authentication (Google)
Your study data: the first name, target score, test date and weekly goal you enter; your answers, time per question, scores, practice results and mistake notebookTo show your scores, progress board, review questions and what to practice next.Cloud Firestore (Google), Mumbai region, linked to your account
Advertising data: the device advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, device information and ad interactionsTo show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud (Google AdMob).Google AdMob

We do not collect your contacts, photos, files, precise location, microphone or camera, or data from other apps. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; HomiLabs never sees or stores them. Only you, signed in as yourself, can read or change your study data, enforced by security rules on the database. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) and Google encrypts it at rest. The app has no analytics.

3. Advertising

Module Up is free and shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. Ads never appear while you answer, read a passage or review a question. We ask Google to limit ads to content rated for teens and older (PG). In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and where else required, you are asked for consent first. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads).

4. Who else handles data

Google acts as our service provider for Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and AdMob. We do not sell personal information and share it with no one else, unless the law requires it. Google may process data in other countries under its own safeguards (see Firebase privacy).

5. How long we keep it

Your account and study data are kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your study data and sign-in account are deleted permanently, within 30 days including backups.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and all study data in Account and settings → Delete account and data. Without the app, follow these steps.
  • Sign out in Account and settings.
  • You may ask us for a copy of your data or ask us to correct or delete it and, where the law gives you these rights, to restrict or object to its use or to complain to your data protection authority. Write to homilabs.smc@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.

7. Children

Module Up is intended for students aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes

We update this policy before a version that changes what the app collects is released. The date above shows the latest change; important changes are also shown in the app.
