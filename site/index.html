Module Up: Digital SAT Prep

Android app by HomiLabs · package com.homilabs.moduleup

Practice for the Digital SAT® with tests that work like the real one. Module Up has 10 full adaptive papers for Reading and Writing and 10 for Math, lessons and practice sets for every skill at four levels, a built-in calculator and reference sheet for Math, and a progress board. Every question is original HomiLabs work.

Quick answers

  • Do I need an account? You can try one practice set as a guest. Full papers and saved progress need a free account (Google or email).
  • Are these real SAT questions? No. Every question is written by HomiLabs in the style of the Digital SAT. Scores are estimates, not official SAT scores.
  • How do I delete my data? Account and settings → Delete account and data. Or follow the email steps.
  • Ads? The app is free and shows Google AdMob ads, never while you answer, read or review a question.
