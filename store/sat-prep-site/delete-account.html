Delete your account and data

Module Up: Digital SAT Prep · Last updated 23 October 2026

You can delete your Module Up account and all your study data at any time. Deletion is permanent.

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Module Up and tap the account icon at the top right to open Account and settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and data.
  3. Confirm with Delete. The app deletes your study data and then your sign-in account. If you are asked to sign in again first, do so and repeat these steps.

Option 2: by email

If you can no longer open the app, send an email to homilabs.smc@gmail.com from the email address of your Module Up account (or of the Google account you used), with the subject "Delete my Module Up data". We delete your account and data and confirm by email within 30 days (usually much sooner). We may ask you to confirm it is you.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email address, and your name and Google account identifier if you used Google) in Firebase Authentication.
  • All your study data in Cloud Firestore: profile (first name, target score, test date, weekly goal), answers, times, scores, practice results and mistake notebook.
  • Backups are cleared within 30 days.

What is not deleted

  • Your Google account itself, and anything Google holds about ads or your use of its services. Manage those in your Google Account.

See the privacy policy for details about what the app collects.
