# Module Up: Digital SAT Prep — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026. App by HomiLabs (homilabs.org). Contact: homilabs.smc@gmail.com

Module Up is a study app for the Reading and Writing section of the Digital SAT®. This policy explains what
the app collects, why, and how you can delete it.

## What we collect
| Data | Why | Where it is stored |
|---|---|---|
| Your account: email address and, if you sign in with Google, your name and Google account ID | To sign you in and keep your progress on any phone | Google Firebase Authentication |
| Your study data: the name, target score, test date and weekly goal you enter; your answers, time per question, scores, practice results and mistake notebook | To show your scores, progress board and review questions | Google Cloud Firestore (Mumbai region), linked to your account |
| Guest use: an anonymous account ID | So you can try a practice set before creating an account | Google Firebase Authentication |
| Advertising data: the device advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and ad interactions | To show ads (Google AdMob), measure them and prevent fraud | Google AdMob |

We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location or any data from other apps. Passwords are handled by
Firebase Authentication; HomiLabs never sees or stores them.

## Advertising
The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads never appear while you answer, read a passage or review a
question. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before showing
personalized ads (Google User Messaging Platform). You can change ad personalization in your device's Google
settings. Google's use of data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

## Sharing
We do not sell your data. Data is processed by Google (Firebase and AdMob) as our service provider and as
described in Google's privacy policy. We share nothing else with anyone, unless the law requires it.

## Security
All data travels encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Firestore rules allow each student to read and write only their own data.

## Children
Module Up is for students aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13.

## Keeping and deleting your data
Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and all study data at any time:
- in the app: **Account and settings → Delete account and data**, or
- on the web: https://homilabs.org/moduleup/delete-account (send the request from your account email).
Deletion is permanent and is completed within 30 days, including backups.

## Changes
If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
